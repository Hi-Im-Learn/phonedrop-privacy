PhoneDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Summary: PhoneDrop is designed primarily for direct local-network transfers. Files, photos, text, and links transferred through PhoneDrop are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PhoneDrop handles information when you use the application.

PhoneDrop allows users to transfer files, photos, text, and links between a Windows PC and other devices, including Android phones and iPhones, primarily through the same local network.

2. Local Network Transfers

PhoneDrop is designed to transfer content directly between your devices over your local network whenever possible.

Content transferred using PhoneDrop may include:

  • Files
  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Text
  • Web links

This transferred content is not intentionally uploaded to or stored on servers operated by the PhoneDrop developer.

3. Information Processed During Use

To provide local device discovery, pairing, and transfer functionality, PhoneDrop may temporarily process technical information such as:

  • Local IP addresses
  • Device or browser information
  • Pairing session identifiers
  • File names
  • File sizes
  • Transfer direction
  • Transfer status
  • Transfer timestamps

This information is used only as necessary to provide, secure, and operate PhoneDrop's functionality.

4. QR Pairing and Sessions

PhoneDrop may use QR codes and temporary session identifiers to pair your phone or browser with your Windows PC.

Pairing tokens and session identifiers are temporary security values used to establish and maintain a connection between devices. PhoneDrop does not intentionally store these values as permanent transfer history.

5. Transfer History

PhoneDrop may store transfer history locally on your Windows PC. Transfer history may include:

  • Transfer time
  • Transfer direction
  • File name
  • File size
  • Transfer type
  • Transfer status
  • Basic device information

PhoneDrop is designed not to store the complete content of transferred text messages or full web links in transfer history by default.

Transfer history is stored locally on the user's Windows PC and can be cleared from within the application.

6. Application Settings

PhoneDrop stores application preferences locally on your computer. These settings may include:

  • Theme preferences
  • Transfer settings
  • Receive folder preferences
  • Notification preferences
  • Local server preferences

These settings are not intentionally uploaded to servers operated by the PhoneDrop developer.

7. Microsoft Store Purchases and Licensing

PhoneDrop may offer free and paid license options, including one-time purchases and subscription plans.

Purchases, subscriptions, payment processing, and license verification may be handled by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store.

PhoneDrop does not receive or store your full payment card details. Microsoft may process information related to purchases, subscriptions, Store accounts, and licenses according to Microsoft's own privacy policies and terms.

8. No Sale of Personal Information

The PhoneDrop developer does not sell personal information obtained through PhoneDrop.

9. No Advertising Tracking

PhoneDrop does not currently use advertising SDKs or behavioral advertising trackers.

If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Network Security

PhoneDrop currently performs local transfers over the local network. Depending on the version and configuration of PhoneDrop, local network communication may use HTTP and may therefore not provide transport encryption.

Users should only use PhoneDrop on networks they trust.

PhoneDrop uses security measures such as temporary pairing tokens, session validation, access controls, request validation, and other protections intended to reduce unauthorized access.

However, no software or network communication method can guarantee absolute security.

11. File Safety

PhoneDrop transfers files selected by users and does not guarantee that transferred files are free from malware or other harmful content.

Users remain responsible for deciding which files to send, receive, open, or execute.

12. Data Retention

PhoneDrop primarily stores settings and transfer-history metadata locally on the Windows PC.

Users can clear transfer history from within the application. Removing PhoneDrop from a device may also remove locally stored application data depending on Windows and Microsoft Store behavior.

13. Third-Party Services

PhoneDrop may rely on operating-system and Microsoft Store services for functionality such as application distribution, licensing, purchases, and updates.

Those services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

14. Children's Privacy

PhoneDrop is a general-purpose file-transfer utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PhoneDrop's features, data practices, legal requirements, or third-party integrations change.

The latest version of this policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
